Reading help in Orlando, Florida.

ClearCode Reading is an evidence-based reading center opening in the Orlando metro area in 2027 for K–8 students who need structured literacy intervention, including children with dyslexia. The ClearCode Reading iOS app for iPhone and iPad shows placement, session progress, specialist notes, and home practice. It supports ClearCode instruction; it does not replace a reading specialist.

Who we serve

Built for Central Florida families looking for real reading intervention.

About two in five Florida students in grades 3–8 read below grade level. ClearCode exists so Orlando-area families can reach evidence-based help without guessing which app, tutor, or center actually teaches reading.

  • K–8 students who need explicit decoding and fluency instruction
  • Children with dyslexia or suspected reading disabilities, as an educational service
  • Families comparing reading centers, tutors, and reading apps in Orlando
  • Parents using Florida education scholarships or private pay

The street address will be published when the Orlando site is finalized. Until then, families from across the Orlando metro area and the rest of Florida can request a consultation or join the waitlist.

Source: Florida Department of Education, 2024–25 FAST ELA/Reading results, grades 3–8.

Methods

Structured literacy, not a new curriculum we invented.

Grades K–2

Orton-Gillingham

Direct, multi-sensory, cumulative instruction used for students with dyslexia and related reading difficulties.

Grades 3–8

Phonics for Reading

Explicit decoding instruction with fluency and comprehension support, based on the Science of Reading.
