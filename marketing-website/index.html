How ClearCode Works

One connected path from placement to progress.

A straightforward process, built around your child. Every step gives specialists sharper insight to guide instruction. You get a clearer view of their progress.

1

Precise Placement

Every child completes an initial assessment and is placed into one structured literacy sequence. K–2 students are placed in Orton-Gillingham; grades 3–8 students are placed in Phonics for Reading.

2

Specialist-Led Sessions

Reading specialists deliver lessons aligned to each program’s methodology and capture student progress in every session.

3

Live Parent Dashboard

Families see skill mastery, fluency trends, specialist notes, next steps, and at-home practice resources.

4

Responsive Support

Each pathway has built-in sessions for review and additional support to ensure skill mastery.
