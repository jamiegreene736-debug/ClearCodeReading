Our Approach

One connected reading path.

A straightforward process, built around your child.

  1. 1

    Precise Placement

    Every child completes an initial assessment and is placed into one structured literacy sequence. K–2 students are placed in Orton-Gillingham; grades 3–8 students are placed in Phonics for Reading.

  2. 2

    Specialist-Led Sessions

    Reading specialists deliver lessons aligned to each program’s methodology and capture student progress in every session.

  3. 3

    Progress you can follow

    Families see skill mastery, fluency trends, specialist notes, next milestones, and connected home practice.
