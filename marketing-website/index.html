Our Approach

One connected reading path.

A straightforward process, built for your child.

  1. 1

    Precise Placement

    An initial assessment places every child into one structured literacy sequence: IMSE Orton-Gillingham Plus for K–2, Phonics for Reading for grades 3–8.

  2. 2

    Specialist-Led Sessions

    Reading specialists teach each program with fidelity and log progress every session. Groups never exceed three students, matched by skill and age.

  3. 3

    Progress you can follow

    Families see skill mastery, fluency trends, specialist notes, next steps, and at-home practice resources.
