The Interventions

Reading intervention that works

Two established programs, both grounded in the Science of Reading and used by reading specialists for decades. We didn't invent the curriculum your child learns from, and that is the point.

Grades K–2

Orton-Gillingham

The approach structured literacy is built on. Direct, multi-sensory, cumulative, and diagnostic-prescriptive, developed specifically for students with dyslexia and related reading difficulties.

Academy of Orton-Gillingham Practitioners and Educators

Grades 3–8

Phonics for Reading

Developed by Dr. Anita Archer, drawing on decades of research into the Science of Reading. Explicit decoding instruction with fluency and comprehension support built in.

Curriculum Associates

How We're Built
