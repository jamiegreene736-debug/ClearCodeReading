About

Every Child Deserves to Read

ClearCode Reading is a scalable center model that makes evidence-based reading intervention accessible to K–8 students, opening in the Orlando metro area.

Why We Exist

The Interventions

Reading intervention that works

Age-appropriate, evidence-based interventions delivered by specialists who know each child.

Grades K–2

IMSE Orton-Gillingham Plus (OG+)

An evidence-based, diagnostic-prescriptive roadmap for struggling readers and students with dyslexia — direct, multi-sensory, cumulative instruction, sequenced by assessment.

Source: Institute for Multi-Sensory Education (imse.com)

Grades 3–8

Phonics for Reading

Dr. Anita Archer’s explicit instruction framework for older readers with severe foundational gaps — explicit phonics and decoding, paired with fluency practice and decodable texts.

Curriculum Associates Research Base (ESSA Level 4)

What We Believe

Four principles

The beliefs that shape how we teach, communicate, remove barriers, and support the specialists doing the work.

01 / GROWTH

Nearly all reading challenges are solvable

Not a matter of intelligence or effort. A specific set of skill gaps, and the right instruction closes them.

02 / CLARITY

Families deserve clarity

Published rates, a clear starting point, and an honest timeline before anyone commits.

03 / ACCESS

Cost shouldn't decide who reads

Florida education scholarships accepted alongside private pay.

04 / EXPERTISE

Specialists deserve better

Above-market pay, profit sharing, and covered certification. Strategy, not charity.
