Before you send

Protect student and account information.

  • Never send a password, access code, full educational record, or payment information.
  • Use a reader’s first name or initials only when identification is necessary.
  • Include your device type, app version, and the steps that led to the problem.
  • Report a suspected security issue using the Security concern topic below.

Common paths

Start with the right kind of help.

Sign-in and account access

If your password is not working or your role looks wrong, tell us the email address on the account. Do not include your password.

Readers and progress

Reader visibility follows center membership, guardian relationships, and consent. Your school or center may need to approve an access change.

Session logging

The iOS app safely queues a session when the network is unavailable. Reconnect and reopen the app before submitting a duplicate.

Notifications

Check iPhone or iPad Settings, select ClearCode Reading, and confirm Notifications are allowed. Notification availability can also depend on your organization.

Privacy choices

Request access, correction, or deletion through the form. We may need to verify your identity or coordinate with the school or center that controls the record.

Read the privacy notice

Security concerns

If you believe an account or record was accessed improperly, report it promptly. Do not include passwords or sensitive records in the message.
