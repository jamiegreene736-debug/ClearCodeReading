Our approach

Structured literacy, taught with expertise and measured with care.

ClearCode gives specialists a connected instructional system while keeping professional judgment, direct teaching, and the child’s learning evidence at the center.

1

One methodology at a time

Each child is placed into Phonics for Reading or IMSE OG+ from methodology-specific evidence. ClearCode does not blend the sequences.

2

Specialist-led intervention

Trained specialists teach, observe, record, and decide. Technology organizes evidence and reduces administrative work.

3

Visible, measurable progress

Structured outcomes, fluency trends, mastery evidence, and specialist notes create a clearer shared view.

Education-only

Reading instruction—not medical care.

ClearCode supports educational assessment, placement, intervention instruction, progress measurement, and family communication. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide therapy for any medical condition.

When a family needs clinical or medical guidance, we encourage them to consult the appropriate licensed professional.
