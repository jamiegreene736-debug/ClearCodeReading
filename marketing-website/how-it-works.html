How it works

Three connected steps. One reading path.

ClearCode places every child in the right structured-literacy sequence, teaches in specialist-led groups of three or fewer, and shows families real progress on a live dashboard.

Precise Placement Specialist-led sessions Progress you can follow

Step 1

Precise Placement

A specialist uses your child’s age, grade band, and methodology-specific assessment evidence to select the program and exact starting point. Each child follows one methodology at a time—never a blended sequence and never pushed past a gap.

Grades K–2

IMSE Orton-Gillingham Plus

Direct, multisensory, cumulative, and diagnostic-prescriptive instruction, with the starting position determined by assessment results.

Grades 3–8

Phonics for Reading

Explicit phonics and decoding instruction paired with fluency practice and comprehension support through decodable text.

Step 2

Specialist-led sessions

Trained specialists lead every intervention session. Fast structured capture records the target, accuracy, observed patterns, mastery signals, next direction, and home-practice suggestion in under five minutes.
