How it works

A clear intervention path, from first placement to every next step.

Your child’s learning evidence determines the program and exactly where they begin. ClearCode keeps placement, instruction, progress, and family communication connected without replacing the expertise of a trained reading specialist.

The Assessment

Understand where the breakdown is happening.

Before instruction begins, your child completes a comprehensive evaluation aligned to the intervention they may receive. It establishes a precise baseline in decoding, word recognition, and spelling, identifies unmastered skills, and gives your family a clear picture of what comes next.

Precise Placement

Start in the right structured-literacy sequence.

A specialist uses your child’s age, grade band, and methodology-specific assessment evidence to select the program and exact starting point. Each child follows one methodology at a time—never a blended sequence and never pushed past a gap.

Grades K–2

Orton-Gillingham

Direct, multisensory, cumulative, and diagnostic-prescriptive instruction, with the starting position determined by assessment results.

Grades 3–8

Phonics for Reading

Explicit phonics and decoding instruction paired with fluency practice and comprehension support through decodable text.

Progress families can understand now—not next month.

Parents see foundational-skill mastery, fluency trends, the latest specialist note, sequence milestones, realistic next steps, and home practice connected to actual sessions. When evidence shows that a skill is not sticking, instruction is adjusted instead of relying on guesswork.
