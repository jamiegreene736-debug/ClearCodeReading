Frequently asked questions

What families usually want to know.

Clear answers about who we serve, how instruction works, and what to expect when you begin.

Who does ClearCode serve?+

ClearCode is designed for K–8 students who need explicit foundational reading intervention. A consultation helps determine whether the model fits the reader’s educational needs.

Is ClearCode a diagnostic or medical service?+

No. ClearCode provides educational reading intervention. It does not diagnose dyslexia, replace medical advice, or guarantee a particular result or timeline.

Which instructional methods are used?+

Students follow one methodology at a time: Orton-Gillingham Plus for the K–2 pathway or Phonics for Reading for the grades 3–8 pathway, based on appropriate placement evidence.

How large are the groups?+

ClearCode groups are designed for a maximum of three students so specialists can deliver direct instruction and observe individual responses.

How will I know whether progress is happening?+

The family dashboard connects skill mastery, fluency trends, recent specialist notes, next milestones, and home practice to session evidence.

How do we begin?+

Submit the short consultation request. The team will review it, contact you, and explain fit, availability, placement, cost, and the clearest next step.
