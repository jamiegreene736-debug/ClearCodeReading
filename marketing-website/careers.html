The Work Itself

We've built the workday you've always envisioned

Structured literacy intervention, delivered the way the research says it should be.

Three Students, Maximum

Sixty-minute sessions with groups of no more than three, grouped by shared skills and age. One-to-one when that's the better fit. Never thirty kids and one of you.

A Program That Backs You

Orton-Gillingham for K–2, Phonics for Reading for grades 3–8. Students arrive already assessed and placed, so you start teaching on day one.

A Platform That Does the Busywork

Session notes, progress tracking, and parent updates happen in one place, built for clinicians rather than bolted on. Less time reconstructing what happened, more time deciding what comes next.

Paid Planning Time

Planning is part of the job, so we pay for it. Your prep happens on the clock, not at your kitchen table after dinner.

Growth & Ownership

Earn, own, and grow

Profit Sharing

Specialists share in center-level profits. When the center succeeds, the clinical team shares in that success. It's a form of ownership public schools structurally cannot offer.

Certification, On Us

Not certified yet? ClearCode covers Orton-Gillingham coursework and provides supervised practicum hours. A 12–18 month pipeline that leaves you with a credential you keep for life.

Clinical Culture

Professional development, clinical supervision, and a caseload built around doing the work well. We are not optimizing for volume.

Compensation

And yes, the pay is better

Great work environments shouldn't require a pay cut. Skilled reading specialists are the scarcest resource in this field, and we compensate accordingly, with profit sharing on top of base pay.
