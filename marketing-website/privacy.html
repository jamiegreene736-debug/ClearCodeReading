Privacy

Student information deserves clear boundaries.

ClearCode is designed for educational intervention with FERPA-oriented practices, meaningful parental consent, and no sale of student data.

This page is a plain-language product overview, not a replacement for applicable agreements or legal notices.

What we collect

Information needed to provide instruction and communicate progress, such as contact details, student learning profile information, placement evidence, session records, mastery and fluency data, and consent history.

Why we use it

To place students accurately, support specialist-led instruction, show authorized families meaningful progress, operate the service, and meet educational recordkeeping responsibilities.

Parental consent and access

We use verified guardian relationships and consent controls before exposing a child’s progress. IDEA-aligned services require the applicable parent and IEP-team approvals in our workflow.

What we do not do

We do not sell student data. We do not use educational records for behavioral advertising. We do not present ClearCode as diagnosis, medical treatment, or therapy.

How we protect access

Access follows the educational relationship.

Center-scoped permissions keep operational records separated.

Guardians see only children connected through approved relationships.

Consent status is checked before protected progress is shown.

Operational access and important changes are auditable.

Questions about privacy?

Use the contact form and select privacy in your message. We’ll route your question to the right person.
