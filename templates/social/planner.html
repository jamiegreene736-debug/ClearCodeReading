How the ideas are chosen

Topics rotate through family reading routines, literacy explained, encouragement, and ClearCode’s approach. OpenAI uses our brand guide, approved public facts, your preferences, and recent captions to keep the content relevant and varied. It does not read student records or CRM contacts.

Each idea gets a second AI editorial check before an image is created or a post is automatically scheduled. An uncertain idea stays in Needs review. AI can make mistakes; every future post remains available to review, edit, or skip.

Both platforms share an original square AI-generated, lifelike image with cheerful warmth and natural light. Facebook gets a fuller caption; Instagram gets a shorter caption. OpenAI usage is billed to the connected API account: normally one image and two text requests per week, with four images for the initial previews. Failed requests may be retried up to three times.
