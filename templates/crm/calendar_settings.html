Where to find your calendar link

Gmail / Google Calendar: Open Google Calendar → Settings → select your calendar → Integrate calendar → copy “Secret address in iCal format”. If your organization hides it, ask your Google Workspace administrator.

Apple Calendar / iCloud: Open your calendar’s sharing options, enable Public Calendar, and copy its sharing link. Anyone holding that link can read that calendar; use a calendar you are comfortable sharing this way.

Use a time zone such as America/New_York or America/Los_Angeles. This applies to all-day events and events without a time zone.
