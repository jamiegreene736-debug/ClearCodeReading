Date overrides

Make an exception for a specific date in {{ profile.blocking_timezone|default:"America/New_York" }}. This replaces its weekly block, including any overnight block carried from the previous day. Google/iCal busy events still apply.

Choose “No block” to remove your local blocks for that date, “Block all day” for a day off, or enter different hours. Saving the same date updates its override.
