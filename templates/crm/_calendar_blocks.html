Overnight blocks and daylight saving

An end time earlier than the start continues into the next day. For example, 10:00 PM–7:00 AM blocks overnight. Use “Block the whole day” for a full day. During daylight-saving changes, repeated clock times are both blocked.

Date exceptions

Use an exception when one date should differ from its weekly hours. It replaces that day’s weekly rule, including an overnight block carried from the previous day. A connected calendar’s busy times still apply.
