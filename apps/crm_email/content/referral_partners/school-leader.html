Who we serve

We serve K–8 students who are reading below grade level. That includes students whose beginning-of-year FAST (PM1) reading report showed Level 1 or Level 2.

What we do

  • A 1:1 evaluation finds exactly where each student needs support.
  • Each student gets evidence-based reading intervention targeted to their specific skill gaps, 1:1 or in groups of up to 3
  • Parents get weekly progress reports.
